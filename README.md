### 🪐 What interests me

I want to understand how we explore other worlds, and how robots can help us investigate them. I am building my foundations in planetary science alongside practical experience with programming, 3D data and autonomous navigation.

My current work follows one question: **How do a destination's scientific goals and environment shape the capabilities an exploration robot needs?**

### 🔭 Featured project — Planetary Robotics Exploration

My learning portfolio connects a comparative study of **the Moon, Mars and Europa** with a guided **RGB-D localisation and mapping project**.

| Step | What I have worked on |
|---|---|
| Understand the destination | Compare scientific questions, environmental constraints and exploration challenges. |
| Build the foundations | Inspect RGB-D data, reconstruct point clouds and practise camera geometry and coordinate transforms. |
| Run and examine an experiment | Use Open3D and RTAB-Map in an existing workflow; record trajectories, maps and evaluation results on the TUM indoor benchmark. |
| Ask the next question | Explore how underwater AUV localisation ideas might connect with swarm coordination. This is a research idea for future investigation. |

The recorded SLAM run processed **573 RGB-D pairs** and saved **548 optimised poses**. My focus is understanding the workflow, checking the results and documenting what I learn.

### 🛠️ What I am learning

- **Python & scientific computing:** working with data, small scripts and plots using NumPy and Matplotlib.
- **3D perception:** RGB-D images, point clouds, camera models and Open3D.
- **Navigation fundamentals:** visual odometry, SLAM, loop closures and trajectory evaluation through practical exercises.
- **Research habits:** reading technical sources, recording assumptions and making experiments easier to follow.

I use documentation, tutorials and AI-assisted coding and debugging as part of my learning, and include tool and data attribution in the project.

### 🌱 Next steps

Strengthen my maths and programming foundations, evaluate trajectories at matched timestamps, and test how reduced visual information affects mapping. Over time, I would like to contribute to a student robotics team and learn how these ideas work on real hardware.
